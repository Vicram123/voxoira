Live captions in your browser

Speak or play audio near your microphone and see large live captions. Use full screen for presentations, classes or accessibility.

Press Start and allow the microphone.

Where live captions help

Use captions in classrooms, meetings, talks and family conversations where someone may not hear well. Place the microphone near the speaker, choose the spoken language, then use full screen and the size slider so the text is readable from across the room.

Limits

Captions come from your browser's speech engine, so accuracy depends on audio quality, accents and language. They are not a certified accessibility service for legal or medical settings.

FAQ

Does it need internet?

Usually yes, because many browsers send speech to an online recognition service.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge and Safari. Firefox does not support it.

Are captions saved?

No. Text is shown on screen only. Use the dictation tool if you want to keep a transcript.
