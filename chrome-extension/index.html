Voxoira Voice Typing for Chrome

Voxoira Voice Typing lets you dictate into any text box on any website. Click into a field, press Alt+Shift+V and speak. There is no account, and Voxoira does not receive or store your speech or text.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

What you get

  • Voice typing in text fields, text areas and most editable boxes on any https page
  • Many speech languages, chosen in the popup, plus optional voice commands
  • Save the latest transcript on your device and download it as a .doc file
  • Share a transcript by email, WhatsApp, Facebook, X or LinkedIn when you choose to
  • A Voxoira floating bar shows when it is listening

How to use it

  • Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  • Click into a text box on any website.
  • Press Alt+Shift+V, or click the toolbar icon and choose Start / stop voice typing.
  • Allow microphone access when Chrome asks, then speak. Finished phrases are typed at the cursor.

Limits

It works on https pages. Chrome's own pages and the Web Store cannot be scripted. Some rich editors, such as Google Docs, handle input in their own way and may ignore inserted text. Speech recognition is provided by Chrome and needs an internet connection. See the privacy policy for details.

No extension needed

You can also use the web tools directly: voice to text, voice translator, audio to text and live captions.

FAQ

Is the extension free?

Yes. There is no account and no subscription.

Does Voxoira store what I say?

No. Speech recognition is done by Chrome, and Voxoira does not collect your speech or text.

Which browsers does it work on?

Chrome. Other Chromium browsers may work but are not tested.

Why does it not work in Google Docs?

Google Docs uses a custom editor that often ignores inserted text. Use Google's own voice typing there.

How do I change the shortcut?

Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts and set your own key for Voxoira Voice Typing.
