Arabic voice to text

Click start, speak Arabic, and your words are typed for you. Free, no sign-up.

Tips for Arabic dictation

  • Text is written right to left. Dialects differ a lot, so choose the country closest to how you speak.
  • Recognition is strongest for Modern Standard Arabic and the major dialects; expect more corrections for regional speech.
  • Use Chrome or Edge, allow microphone access, and speak at a steady pace in a quiet room.

Questions

Is Arabic voice to text free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs through your browser’s built-in speech recognition.

Which browser works best for Arabic?

Chrome and Edge give the most reliable results. Some other browsers do not support speech recognition.
