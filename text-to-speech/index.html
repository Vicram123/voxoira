Text to speech online

Paste or type text and listen to it read aloud. It uses the voices already installed on your device, so it is free and private.

How it works

Your browser's built-in speech synthesis reads the text, so nothing is sent to a server. The voices you see depend on your operating system and browser: Chrome, Edge and Safari usually offer many languages. Choose a voice that matches the language of your text for the best pronunciation.

Tips

  • Break very long text into paragraphs; the tool reads it sentence by sentence.
  • Lower the speed for study material and raise it for quick proofreading.
  • Use it to proofread: hearing a draft read aloud makes errors easier to notice.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up or limits.

Why are there few voices?

Voices come from your device. Installing extra language packs in your system settings adds more.

Can I download the audio?

Not yet. This tool plays the speech in your browser.
