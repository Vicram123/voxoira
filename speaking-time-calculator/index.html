Typical speaking speeds

Most people speak between 110 and 160 words per minute. Presentations and audiobooks tend to run slower, around 120 to 150, while conversation is often faster. Pauses, slides and questions add time, so build in a buffer of 10 to 15 percent.

How to use the estimate

  • Read your script aloud once and time it, then compare with the estimate.
  • Trim around 130 words per minute of overage to fit a time limit.

FAQ

How many words is a 5-minute speech?

About 650 words at an average pace of 130 words per minute.

Does it count punctuation?

It counts words separated by spaces.

Is my text saved?

No. Everything stays in your browser.
