Transcribing MP3 files

MP3 is a compressed format, typically about 1 MB per minute at 128 kbps. Speech survives that compression well, so podcasts, interviews, lectures and recorded calls usually transcribe cleanly. Use the original file rather than a re-compressed copy, and pick the Base model when accuracy matters more than speed.

Long files take longer, since everything runs on your own computer. For recordings over an hour, split the file first. Need subtitles? Export SRT or VTT after transcribing.

Questions

Can I transcribe an MP3 without uploading it?

Yes. The file is decoded and transcribed inside your browser. Only the speech model is downloaded once and then cached.

Why is my MP3 slow to transcribe?

Speed depends on your device. The Tiny model is fastest and Base is more accurate but slower. Long recordings take proportionally longer.
