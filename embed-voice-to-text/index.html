Embed a voice-to-text widget

Add a free dictation box to your own site or blog with one line of HTML. Visitors can speak and copy the text.

Copy this code:

<iframe src="https://voxoira.com/embed/?lang=en-US" width="100%" height="320" allow="microphone" style="border:1px solid #ddd" title="Voice to text"></iframe>

Preview

Options

  • lang: a language code such as es-ES, fr-FR or de-DE.
  • theme=dark: dark appearance.
  • The allow="microphone" attribute is required so the browser can ask for microphone access.

Terms of use

The widget is free to use. Please keep the “Powered by Voxoira” link visible. It works in Chrome, Edge and Safari; Firefox visitors will see a short notice.

FAQ

Is the embed free?

Yes, with the Powered by Voxoira link kept visible.

Does it store visitor speech?

No. Recognition uses the visitor's browser and nothing is sent to Voxoira.

Why does the microphone not start?

Your page must be served over HTTPS and the iframe needs the allow="microphone" attribute.
