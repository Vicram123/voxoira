Share a transcript by link

Paste a transcript or note and get a link anyone can open. The text is compressed into the link, so nothing is stored on a server.

How it works

Your text is compressed in your browser and placed in the part of the URL after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to the server, so Voxoira never receives or stores your text. Anyone with the full link can read it, so do not share sensitive material.

Limits

Very long texts produce very long links, which some chat apps and email tools may shorten or break. Use it for notes, short transcripts and snippets.

FAQ

Is the text stored anywhere?

Only inside the link. Voxoira has no server copy.

Can I delete a shared link?

You cannot revoke a link already sent, because the content is the link itself.

Does it work in all browsers?

It needs a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
