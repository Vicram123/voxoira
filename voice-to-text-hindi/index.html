Hindi voice to text

Click start, speak Hindi, and your words are typed for you. Free, no sign-up.

Tips for Hindi dictation

  • Your speech is written in Devanagari script, so you can type Hindi without a Hindi keyboard.
  • Mixing English words into Hindi sentences is common; expect some of them to appear in Devanagari or Latin script.
  • Use Chrome or Edge, allow microphone access, and speak at a steady pace in a quiet room.

Questions

Is Hindi voice to text free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs through your browser’s built-in speech recognition.

Which browser works best for Hindi?

Chrome and Edge give the most reliable results. Some other browsers do not support speech recognition.
